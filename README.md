# Al Mankal Chicken Tikka, Amman — preview site

Showcase site for Al Mankal Chicken Tikka (Gardens and Sweifieh, Amman; owner Saif Khuffash), built by FellowFlow. **Preview only**: `noindex` (meta tags, `_headers`, `robots.txt`) until Saif approves the site.

- Plain HTML, CSS and JS, no build step. Pages: `index.html` (home: order card, menu rail, grill film, reels, branches, story), `menu.html` (every category with search), `branches.html`, `franchise.html`, `404.html`. The five pages share one header, footer, call sheet, basket drawer and bottom bar.
- **Direction "Sufra" (29 October 2026).** Chosen by the founders from the market research (three directions, B recommended): a daylight, menu-first homepage. Cream page, the logo's own red and yellow, the teal of the restaurant's table photos, char for the film band and footer. Type: Barlow Condensed 800 (Latin display), Lalezar (Arabic display), Rubik (Latin text), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Arabic text), all SIL OFL and self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`.
- Every word and fact lives in `assets/js/content.js` (English and Arabic). Facts marked `CONFIRM` wait for Saif. `HOURS` there is empty until Saif gives opening hours; the branch cards say "to confirm" and turn into "Open now / Closed now" (Amman time) once it is filled. The story chapter is a draft.
- The menu is `assets/js/menu-data.js`: the restaurant's own MakanE catalogue (73 dishes, names, descriptions, prices), read from almankal.com on 28 October 2026. Regenerate it when the menu changes (see `tools/`). `PHOTOS` in `content.js` maps a dish to one of the restaurant's Instagram photos in `assets/img/s/` (AVIF and WebP, 480 and 960 px); promo posters are never used as dish photos, and a dish without a photo shows the logo tile.
- **Ordering stays on MakanE.** "Add to basket" keeps a basket in `localStorage` (`mankal-basket`); Checkout shows a hand-over screen that opens the first dish's page on the restaurant's store (`https://almankal.com/products/<slug>`) and offers to copy the list, because MakanE has no API to pre-fill a basket. Delivery or Pickup and Gardens or Sweifieh are saved as `mankal-mode` and `mankal-branch`; the Sweifieh switch opens the Sweifieh store (`jordan.almankal.com`), whose dish addresses are still to confirm. "Order again" shows the last basket sent to checkout from this phone (`mankal-last`).
- **Logo.** `assets/img/s/logo-{124,380,760}.webp` is the restaurant's own mark on its yellow disc (made from `brand/logo-on-yellow.png`, white fills kept): intro, header, footer, 404 and the no-photo tile.
- **Motion.** The entry, once per tab session (`sessionStorage` `mankal-intro`), is "born from the coals": near-dark, one ember wakes in the restaurant's own bed of charcoal (`assets/img/s/bed-{cold,1,2,3}.webp`, cut from `grill-tall.mp4`) and breathes; at half a second the coals catch from it (the hot bed shows through a round window that grows from the ember while the coals shrink by the same amount, transform only; three real frames cross-fade so the flicker is the film's own), the glow rises, the fire film (the same `assets/video/flame*.mp4` as the hero, screen-blended and masked to a soft oval) climbs out of the bed with a few sparks on a small canvas; at one second a warm halo rises from the fire and the emblem comes into its light where it already stands; at two it holds with one slow pulse; at 2.7 the whole scene brightens into the page (opacity, a 5% grow), landing on the hero's own charcoal. Skip sits in the corner; the film loads from the first byte and fades in whenever it can play, so a slow line still gets coals, glow and sparks. Then the hero assembles: the mankal settles in, the words rise in turn, the order card lands. The hero's slot is the mankal itself, the brazier the restaurant is named after (`#hero-mankal`): a charcoal bowl drawn in the page's own black, the real coals from the grill film in it (three frames cross-fading), and a fire that is a film, not a live shader: `assets/video/flame.mp4` (phones get `flame-sm.mp4`, `flame.webm` without H.264) is a volumetric fire rendered offline (ray-marched noise with a black-body colour ramp and a bloom pass, an 8-second seamless loop on black, the tool is kept outside the repo) and screen-blended over the coals, so it costs a phone only a video decode. Live sparks rise from it on a small canvas, the front half of the rim sits over its base, and the name المنقل is written above in ember: the letters are drawn as a stroke, then fill with the ember gradient. It plays only while on screen and after the curtain; a hand or finger on it fans the coals (the flame stretches, the film runs faster, more sparks) and it fans once by itself as the order card lands. With Reduce Motion on, the still frame of the fire (`assets/img/s/flame-still.webp`) sits on the coals and the name is already written. In the headline the word COALS is cut from charcoal and lit from below (a static ember gradient in the letters and a brighter copy that breathes on top, opacity only) with sparks off its baseline; under it the fire line draws a bed of coals from 1985 to today, ending in an ember that keeps glowing and a "still on" label; that ember then jumps to the Call button, which rings once. Category switches deal the cards in; "Add" flies the dish photo into the basket button; the grill band plays the restaurant's own footage (`assets/video/grill-wide` or `grill-tall`, chosen by screen shape) under a warm glow that breathes at the foot of the band and a canvas of embers rising from it (additive blending, halos on the heavy sparks, a slow wind and flicker; fewer on phones, paused when off screen); "1985" counts up; the "Open now" dot pulses; reels play muted when in view, tap for sound; the bottom bar slides up after the intro. Everything animates transform and opacity only; heavy media starts after the curtain. Every dark chapter fades in from the cream above it and out again (gradient bridges), so no colour change is abrupt. With Reduce Motion on, the intro is short, the letters, line, embers and glow are still, and reveals are instant.
- **Complete your sufra.** Above Checkout the basket offers, in groups, what a good waiter would ask: make it a meal (the store's own two-sandwich meals, shown as the price difference), sauces and dips at 0.50, another sandwich (or one for someone at the table, or the chicken when only sides are in), and cold and fresh (drink, salad, bread, fries the order lacks). Meals and offers whose store description already lists cola, dip or bread are not offered them again. Everything is the restaurant's own menu at its own prices. Rules in `site.js` (`UP`, `PAIR`, `renderBasket`); one tap adds the item and the row refreshes.
- Language: `assets/js/lang.js` picks Arabic for Arabic browsers on the first visit, otherwise English; the header button switches and remembers the choice (`mankal-lang`).
- Every release that changes the CSS or JS bumps the `?v=` number on their links in the HTML pages, so visitors never get new HTML with cached old styles.

Preview: https://al-mankal-amman.pages.dev (Cloudflare Pages from `main`).

## Quality pass

`NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node tools/shoot.cjs ../shots` serves the site locally, blocks outside requests, captures every page at 375 px and 1280 px in English and Arabic, exercises the rail, basket, hand-over, call sheet and menu search, and reports console errors, sideways scroll and stray "undefined" text. It must print `no problems`.

## At launch

Remove the `noindex` meta tags, the `X-Robots-Tag` line in `_headers` and the `Disallow` in `robots.txt`; point `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` at `almankal.com`; add `og:image`; fill `HOURS`; replace the hero and dish photos with the restaurant's own where they are still missing.
