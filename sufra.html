The mankal

Charcoal, not gas

  • Coals

    Every piece of chicken at Al Mankal goes over live charcoal. The smoke is the seasoning, and it has been since 1985.

  • Chicken

    Whole chickens cut and pressed on the grate, sold by the piece: a quarter, a half, or a tray for the family.

  • Puri

    A thin dough dropped in the fryer until it balloons. Every sandwich is wrapped in it and every meal comes with it.

  • Sides

    Garlic dip, hummus, Turkish salad and biryani rice with curry sauce. The table is not complete without them.

The story

It started with one grill

In 1985 a charcoal grill was lit in Amman and the first chicken tikka went on it. The smoke drew a queue, and the queue never really left.

Forty-one years later the coals are still lit the same way, in two branches, for the same city.
